t018 ---- unique tags

#t018-frontmatter-tag

This page links to tags that no other t018 pages use, including tags with accents and in other scripts, and tags right after punctuation. Its frontmatter adds the tag t018-frontmatter-tag, which is shown as a tag link at the top of the page, as Obsidian shows frontmatter tags.
